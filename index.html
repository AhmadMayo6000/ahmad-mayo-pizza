Our Story

Founded by Ahmad Mayo, our pizzeria brings the authentic taste of Naples to your neighborhood. We believe that great pizza starts with great ingredients - from our hand-kneaded dough to our vine-ripened tomatoes and premium mozzarella.

Every pizza is crafted with passion, baked in our traditional stone oven, and served with a smile. It's not just food; it's an experience.
